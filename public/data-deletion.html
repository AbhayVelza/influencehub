InfluenceHub Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Velza Global India

You can ask us to delete the information InfluenceHub holds about you, including public Page or profile data we received through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or YouTube.

How to request deletion

  1. Email [privacy contact email] with the subject line "InfluenceHub data deletion".
  2. Include the Facebook Page, Instagram handle or YouTube channel the request is about, and the email address we used to contact you (if any).
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from that account or email so we know it is really you.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt within 7 days.
  • We delete your profile, contact details, metrics, metric history, documents and notes from InfluenceHub within 30 days, and send you a confirmation.
  • Copies in encrypted backups expire within 35 days of deletion.
  • We keep only what the law requires us to keep (for example, signed contracts or invoices), and nothing more.

Removing access from Facebook

If you connected a Facebook account or Page to InfluenceHub, you can also remove the app at any time. In Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find InfluenceHub and choose Remove. Then email us as above to delete data we already stored.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle data.
