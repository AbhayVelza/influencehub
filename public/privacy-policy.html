InfluenceHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Operated by Velza Global India ("Velza", "we", "us")

InfluenceHub is an internal creator-relationship tool used by the Velza team to find, contact and work with social media creators. This policy explains what information InfluenceHub collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Public creator and Page information. Through the Meta Graph API (including Page Public Metadata Access), the Instagram API and the YouTube Data API, we read publicly available information. That includes Page or channel name, username or handle, profile picture, category, follower and subscriber counts, and public post metrics such as reach, likes and views. We do not read private messages, private posts or any information that is not public.

Information creators give us. If you work with us as a creator, we store the contact details you share with us (name, email, phone number, location, content category), signed agreements, account insight documents you choose to send, and the shipping details we need for Welcome Kits.

Team member information. For Velza staff who sign in to InfluenceHub, we store name, work email, role and activity logs (for example, who changed a creator's stage and when).

2. How we use information

  • To find and evaluate creators for brand collaborations.
  • To contact creators, manage onboarding, agreements and Welcome Kit shipping.
  • To show combined analytics (reach, likes, follower growth) for creators who work with us.
  • To keep InfluenceHub secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Data from Meta platforms

Data we receive from Meta is used only for the purposes above and in line with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Public Page metadata is refreshed about once every 7 days. Between refreshes, InfluenceHub shows the last saved values.

4. Sharing

Information is visible only to authorised Velza team members, according to their role. We use service providers to host and run InfluenceHub (cloud hosting and database providers). They process data only on our instructions. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in a secured PostgreSQL database with access limited to authorised staff.
  • Historical metric snapshots are kept for 365 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Creator records are kept while we have, or are discussing, a working relationship, and are deleted on request (see section 7).

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access, encrypted backups and access logging to protect information. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected people of a breach where the law requires.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to our use of it. For how to request deletion, see our Data Deletion Instructions. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

InfluenceHub is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information about children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
